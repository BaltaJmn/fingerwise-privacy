Fingerwise

Privacy

Last updated 15 November 2026. Fingerwise is made by Baltasar Jimenez Rincon, an individual developer in Spain.

Short version: your camera picture never leaves your iPhone, and the app has no account.

The camera

Fingerwise uses the front camera only while a practice screen is open. Each frame is passed to Apple's Vision framework on the device, which returns the position of 21 points on your hand. The app compares those points with each letter and discards the frame. No photo or video is saved, and neither pictures nor hand positions are sent anywhere. If you turn the camera off for Fingerwise in Settings, you can still read every drawing and description.

What stays on your iPhone

The letters you have had ticked, the name you typed to practise, and the left-hand switch are stored in the app's own storage on the device. Deleting the app deletes them. They are not synced or uploaded.

Purchases

Fingerwise Pro is sold through Apple's App Store; Apple handles the payment and the app never sees your card or your Apple Account details. To keep count of sales, the app uses the RevenueCat SDK, which receives the purchase record from the App Store (product, date, country, an anonymous identifier made for this app). It does not receive your name, email, camera data or anything you do in the app. RevenueCat's policy: revenuecat.com/privacy.

What there is not

No account, no advertising, no analytics of how you use the app, no tracking across apps or websites.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects nothing from anyone.
