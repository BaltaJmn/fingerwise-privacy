Fingerwise

The ASL alphabet, checked on your own hand.

Fingerwise is an iPhone app for practising American Sign Language fingerspelling. You hold a letter up to the front camera; the app draws your hand over the picture, ticks the letter when the shape is right and, when it is not, tells you the one thing to change: straighten your ring finger, tuck your thumb in.

Everything happens on your iPhone. The camera picture is analysed and thrown away. There is no account and nothing is uploaded.

What you can practise

  • Each of the 26 letters, with a drawing and a description.
  • The alphabet in a row.
  • Words, from three lists or typed by you, and your own name.

What a camera cannot see

Some letters look almost the same from the front: M, N, S and T differ only in where the thumb hides. Fingerwise says which letters it checks with confidence and which it checks loosely, with the numbers, inside the app. J and Z are movements and are followed roughly.

Fingerspelling is a small part of ASL. To learn the language, learn from Deaf teachers.
